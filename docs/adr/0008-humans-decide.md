# ADR-0008: Humans decide; the CTO Agent recommends

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-21

## Context

An agent cannot be the accountable decision-maker.

## Decision

Every assignment has a named human sponsor and deputy. Every binding approval is theirs and is recorded with their sign-in identity. The CTO Agent's recommendation appears on the decision card and is never applied automatically. It takes part only in Major assignments.

## Consequences

- Accountability is always a person.
- Approved by the repository owner on 21 November 2026.
