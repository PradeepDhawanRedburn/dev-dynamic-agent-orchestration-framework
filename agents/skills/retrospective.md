# Retrospective

## Skill text

```text
SKILL: RETROSPECTIVE
- Work from the record: plan, ledger, test reports, review findings, human decisions. Not from impressions.
- Record what was planned against what happened: cost, time, revisions, rejected results, suspensions.
- For each thing that went wrong, find the earliest point it could have been caught.
- A lesson is specific and usable: the situation, what to do differently, and where that change belongs (a prompt, a profile, a skill, a standard, a check).
- Propose changes; do not make them. Each goes through a pull request.
- Store only approved findings as reusable. Mark the data classification of everything stored.
```
