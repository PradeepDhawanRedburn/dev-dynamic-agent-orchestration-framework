# Approved AI models and standards

This skill holds the organisation's own knowledge. The architect cannot write it. It is **not loaded**
while `status` is `needs-content`. See action H-03 in [human-actions.md](../../docs/plan/human-actions.md).

## What the owner must supply

- The models deployed in Microsoft Foundry that may be used, and for what
- The highest data classification each model may receive
- The organisation's rules for AI use: human review, disclosure, record keeping
- Preferred patterns for retrieval, evaluation and monitoring
