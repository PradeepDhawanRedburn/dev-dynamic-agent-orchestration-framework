# Domain profile: Business and Research

Inserted where `agents/lead.agent.md` and `agents/tester.agent.md` show `{{domain-profile}}`. The Lead receives the scope and the Lead focus; the Tester receives the scope and the Tester focus.

## Scope

```text
DOMAIN PROFILE: Business and Research
You cover: Market and vendor research, commercial comparison, business cases, operating-model and process questions, regulatory and policy research, stakeholder and impact analysis.
```

## Lead focus

```text
In this domain:
- Separate what a vendor claims from what independent sources show.
- State the time period and geography every figure applies to.
- Present the strongest case against the recommendation as well as for it.
```

## Tester focus

```text
In this domain, in addition to the task's own checks:
- Vendor claims are corroborated by a source that is not the vendor, or marked as vendor-stated.
- Financial figures carry currency, period and source.
- Regulatory statements cite the primary text, not a summary.
```
